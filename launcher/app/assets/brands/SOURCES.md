# Brand artwork sources

PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Xbox, Nintendo Switch, YouTube, Netflix, Plex,
Crunchyroll, Amazon Prime, Spotify, Max, Apple TV and Stremio:
Simple Icons 11.15.0 (CC0). https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.15.0/icons
See SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.md. SVG path geometry is preserved; fills are recolored
white (Netflix red) for contrast against the tile background.

Apps without a mark here (GeForce NOW, LG Channels, Globoplay, Hot IPTV, Boosteroid
and any app installed later) show their own launcher icon, read from the TV at run
time by the WebOS TV root helper; no copy of it ships with the app.

HBO Max (hbomax.png): the current (2025) official wordmark, cropped from a press
image at macmagazine.com.br. Used (as a raster, full-bleed badge via
APP_ICON_OVERRIDES, not APP_LOGOS) for the installed app `com.wbd.stream`, whose
own device-bundled icon.png is an outdated pre-rebrand asset.

Disney+ 2024: Disney Enterprises, Inc., via Wikimedia Commons (PD-textlogo).
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Disney%2B_2024.svg

Wikimedia SVGs are also recolored white. Brand names and logos belong to their
respective owners and identify the destination apps/devices; no endorsement is implied.

- webostvplayer.png: own artwork for WebOS TV Player (the IPTV player in ../../player), drawn for this project.
